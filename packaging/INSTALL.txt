Installing Lucky Seven
======================

The easiest way is the installer: download Lucky-Seven-<version>.pkg from
https://github.com/tomdmaguire/lucky-seven/releases/latest and open it.
These steps are for installing by hand from this zip instead.

1. Copy the plugins into place:
   - "Lucky Seven.vst3"      -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   - "Lucky Seven.component" -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (AU, for Logic and GarageBand)

   In Finder, press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the folder path to open it.

2. Unblock the plugins. Lucky Seven is free and not signed by an Apple developer
   account, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Lucky Seven.vst3"
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Lucky Seven.component"

   Alternatively, if your DAW reports the plugin as blocked, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Allow Anyway".

3. Get the impulse responses. The easiest way: open Lucky Seven and click
   "Download Bricasti IRs" on its first screen. To do it by hand, download
   Samplicity's free Bricasti M7 archive,
   "Samplicity - Bricasti IRs version 2023-10.zip" (964 MB):
   https://cdn.samplicity.com/downloads/Samplicity%20-%20Bricasti%20IRs%20version%202023-10.zip
   (If that link stops working, it's the "Bricasti M7 impulse response archive"
   at the very bottom of https://www.samplicity.com/downloads/.)
   Unzip it and move the whole folder into ~/Music/Lucky Seven/IRs (the folder
   is created the first time the plugin opens).

4. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW's settings, or restart it.
