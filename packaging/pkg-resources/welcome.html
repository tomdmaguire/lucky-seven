Lucky Seven

A free true-stereo convolution reverb with a decay control and a sound map of every program. It works with Samplicity's free Bricasti M7 impulse responses, downloaded from Samplicity the first time you open it, and with any impulse responses of your own.

Lucky Seven is an independent project, not affiliated with Bricasti Design or Samplicity. No impulse responses are included in this installer.

This installs the VST3 and Audio Unit versions for all users. You can choose which formats to install on the next screens.
